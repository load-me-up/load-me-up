An example essay

1 January 2026

A sample you can copy when you start a new piece. The column, the poem and the notes are the pattern. The sentences are only here to fill it.

This is placeholder text. It is not a diary, and it is not about a person or a place. It is here so the page can be read as a page, and then replaced with your own essay.

The measure is about sixty-five characters. That length is calm for a long piece: the eye finds the next line without a search. On a phone the column narrows to the screen, with a margin on either side. The type size grows a little as the window gets wider, and the space between lines stays generous.

Italics are for a light stress, or for a title such as A Sample Page. Bold is there if you ever need it. Keep quotations curly, “like this”, and keep apostrophes curly, as in don’t. Where a sentence breaks off and starts again, a spaced en dash – like this – is the mark to use.

A block quote holds a line you want read twice. Keep it to one or two sentences, and let the rule at the left do the signalling.

The notes at the end are for an aside that would clog the paragraph. A marker sits up on the line, and the note waits below.1

The three asterisks are a section break. In the file it is an ordinary horizontal rule. The stylesheet draws the ornament, so every break on the site looks the same.

A poem can sit in the middle of an essay. The lines below are a pattern for that, not a finished poem. Type one line per line, and leave a blank line before a new stanza.

Write the line, then break it. Leave the next one underneath, as you would on paper. A blank line starts a new stanza. The gap is the only marker you need. Then carry on down the page.

Small caps are a class on a span, as with this short label. The same pattern covers a flourish, once the font has it: discretionary ligatures, a swash, or stylistic set one.2
